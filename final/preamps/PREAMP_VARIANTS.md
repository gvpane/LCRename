# Preamp Variants & Impedance Settings Guide

The Focusrite Liquid Channel meticulously sampled various hardware units at different impedance settings and drive levels. The alphabetical variants (A, B, C, D) and modifiers (H, P) correspond directly to how the original gear was configured during the sampling process.

## General Modifiers
*   **H (Hot)**: The preamp was driven hard with a high input gain setting to induce harmonic distortion/saturation.
*   **P (Pad) / PAD**: The preamp was recorded with its padded input setting engaged.

## Specific Hardware Variants

### AVALON AD 2022 (`SILVER 1`)
*   **A**: Impedance set to 'mic'
*   **B**: Impedance set to 'line'
*   **C**: Impedance set to 150 Ohms (Low)
*   **D**: High impedance setting (mic) and driven hard (HOT)
*   **E**: Medium impedance setting (600 Ohms) and driven hard (HOT)
*   **F**: Low impedance setting (150 Ohms) and driven hard (HOT)

### UNIVERSAL AUDIO M610 (`STELLAR 1`)
*   **A**: High impedance setting
*   **B**: Low impedance setting
*   **C**: High impedance setting and driven hard (HOT)
*   **D**: Low impedance setting and driven hard (HOT)

### DW FEARN VT-2 (`SCARLET`)
*   *(Standard)*: Normal impedance and gain
*   **B**: Driven hard (HOT)
*   **C**: Low impedance setting and driven hard (HOT)

### dbx 786 (`BIG BLUE`)
*   **A**: High impedance setting
*   **B**: Low impedance setting

### GROOVE TUBES VIPRE (`BIG TUBE`)
*   **A**: Rise Time Slow setting (Low impedance)
*   **B**: Rise Time Slow setting (Medium impedance)

### MILLENNIA STT-1 (`NEW AGE 2`)
*   **A**: Vacuum tube topology with no transformer
*   **B**: Vacuum tube topology with transformer
*   **E**: Solid state setting
*   **F**: Solid state setting and transformer input

### NEVE 1073 (`CLASS A 2`)
*   **A**: High impedance setting
*   **B**: Low impedance setting

### AMS NEVE 1081 (`CLASS AB3`)
*   **A**: High impedance setting
*   **B**: Low impedance setting

### NEVE 33114 (`CLASS A 1`)
*   *(Standard)*: Transformer-based Mic Pre and EQ
*   **B**: Transformer-based Mic Pre and EQ, driven hard (HOT)

*(Source: Focusrite Liquid Channel Official PDF Documentations (2004-2006))*
